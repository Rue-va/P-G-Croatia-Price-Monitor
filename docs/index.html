How the numbers are calculated
  • Source: each retailer's legally mandated public daily price list (NN 75/2025), one file per store, collected automatically every morning.
  • Same product, different retailer: SKUs matched by barcode. Each retailer's price is the median regular price across its stores. The price index takes, for every shared SKU, that retailer's price ÷ the cheapest retailer's price, then the median across SKUs.
  • Category shelf: each dot is one SKU's median price per unit (per wash for Laundry/Fabric Enhancers, otherwise per litre, kg or piece as published by the retailer). Packs priced in different units are shown separately — switch with the unit buttons.
  • TDP (total distribution points): for each product, the number of stores that list it, added up across products. A brand with 10 products each in 50 stores has a TDP of 500. It combines range and reach in one number, so it shows who has the wider distribution.
  • Store check: a product is in the chain's core range if at least half of that retailer's stores list it today.
  • Missing SKUs: a P&G SKU a store listed before but has left off its price list for 3+ consecutive days on which that store did publish. Worth checking with the store — the product may also have been delisted. "Was on promotion" means the product had a promo price the last day it was listed: it most likely sold out during the promotion or was a promo-only listing.
  • Promotions: rows with a published special-sale price. Where the retailer leaves the regular price blank (Konzum), it is estimated from the same SKU's non-promo price in other stores (marked *).
Data: retailers' public daily price lists (NN 75/2025). Missing-SKU signals are inferred from price-list absence, not warehouse inventory.
