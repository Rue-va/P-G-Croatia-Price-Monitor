How the numbers are calculated
  • Source: each retailer's legally mandated public daily price list (NN 75/2025), one file per store.
  • Price position: for each brand, the median price per unit across all its store listings (in the category's most common unit, so €/piece toothbrushes are never averaged with €/l toothpaste). P&G's figure is the average of its brands' medians, likewise for competitors; the cell shows how far P&G sits above (+) or below (−) competitors.
  • Promotions are rows where the retailer publishes a special-sale price below the regular price.
  • Price changes compare the regular price of the same SKU in the same store against the previous published day, so stores that didn't publish can't create fake changes.
  • Possible gaps are SKUs missing from a store's list for 3+ consecutive days on which that store did publish. That's a signal to check, not proof of out-of-stock — the product may also have been delisted.
